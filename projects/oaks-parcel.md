## Description

Oak's Parcel is a modern iOS take on a Pokedex app, completely free with no features behind a paywall. It covers main-line Pokemon games from Generations I–VII.

Features as of its last update:

- Details on the first 807 Pokemon — sprites for different forms/varieties, search by name/type/generation, moves, stats, abilities, and evolutions
- Details on all moves, abilities, items, and locations, including encounters
- Save favorite Pokemon, moves, abilities, and items for quick access
- Build custom teams
- Catch-tracking checklists styled after the in-game PC Box
- Damage calculator (moves, EVs, IVs, natures, abilities)
- Type matchup chart

[View on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/oaks-parcel/id1523406646)
