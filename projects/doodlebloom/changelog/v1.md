## 1.0 — In beta

{% build 9, "2026-10-19" %}
- New: Four lighter app icons: Apricot, Butter, Pistachio and Blush.
- New: App Icon has its own page in Settings, showing each icon on a light and a dark Home Screen side by side.
- Improved: In dark mode, every app icon shares the same dark background, and its colour moves into the squiggle.
- Improved: When your devices are in sync, one nightly backup covers them all. Devices that aren't in sync yet each keep backing up until they match.
- New: Settings › Backup can clear backups from devices that stopped backing up, after 30, 60 or 90 days, or never. Each device's newest backup is always kept.
{% endbuild %}

{% build 8, "2026-10-14" %}
- New: Drawings can no longer be lost to iCloud sync. When a device sends an older copy of a page, like a blank one from before the drawing reached it, DoodleBloom puts your drawing back.
- New: When a day was drawn on two devices before they synced, both drawings are kept, and DoodleBloom asks right away which to keep. Keep one, keep both as separate pages, or combine them into one page.
- New: Nightly backups. While your device charges, DoodleBloom saves a backup to its folder in iCloud Drive, keeping the last 7 from each device. Restore one from Settings › Backup.
- Improved: The tint colour now starts as Forest, and reaches every part of the app, including text cursors and menus.
{% endbuild %}

{% build 7, "2026-10-09" %}
- Improved: On iPhone Duo unfolded and iPad in landscape, Year in Bloom opens full screen with the title beside the poster. A month, or the whole year, fits on one screen, and collages use more columns.
- Improved: Built with the iOS 27.1 SDK, ready for iPhone Duo.
{% endbuild %}

{% build 6, "2026-10-08" %}
- New: Back up and restore, in Settings. A backup keeps every page and Studio piece with its Apple Pencil strokes, so restored drawings stay editable, along with your settings. It's free.
- New: When a restored day already has a different page, you choose which to keep, now or later from Settings.
- Improved: Restore Purchases says what it found.
- Fixed: Subscribers no longer see the Premium offer flash for a moment when the app opens.
{% endbuild %}

{% build 5, "2026-10-08" %}
- Fixed: Sharing a drawing, a Year in Bloom poster or a replay to apps like Instagram's messages no longer fails with "Can't send file".
{% endbuild %}

{% build 4, "2026-10-07" %}
- New: Year in Bloom can show favourites only, for a month or a whole year, and in what you share.
- New: Sharing a Year in Bloom poster offers an Automatic, Light or Dark page, and the poster on screen matches.
- Improved: Year in Bloom's calendar shows every day as a numbered tile, with drawings in their own colours and today outlined in your tint colour.
- Improved: On screen, the year view shows larger months, and tapping one opens it.
- Improved: Collages size their columns to the number of drawings, and the columns end at about the same height.
- Improved: An empty month says so and offers today's page, instead of a blank poster.
- Improved: Clearer small print on the poster, and loading no longer makes the poster jump.
- Fixed: On the iPad mini and iPad Air 11", the Journal's buttons no longer hide behind a "…" button.
{% endbuild %}

{% build 3, "2026-10-05" %}
- New: Days are grouped under month headers in the Journal, with shorter dates on each drawing.
- New: On iPad, the Sketchbook shows two-page spreads, and a page's ⋯ menu can draw a day across both pages.
- New: A Tint colour setting under Appearance, with ten colours for buttons, links and tabs.
- New: A finished day offers "Keep drawing in the Studio" instead of drawing tools.
- Improved: Year in Bloom has its own button, and opens on this month, with a Month | Year switch.
- Improved: List rows are all the same height, with one heart and the page's title or prompt under its date.
- Improved: On iPhone, undo and redo float on the page, so the date fits in the top bar.
- Improved: Share appears as soon as you draw. One-finger panning now uses Draw with Finger in the tool palette.
- Improved: Settings are regrouped, and the reminder offer waits for a later visit and never returns once answered.
- Improved: A calmer share sheet, a bigger replay, and a lighter Premium sheet with one close button.
- Fixed: The first copy into the Studio could open blank.
{% endbuild %}

{% build 2, "2026-10-02" %}
- New: A Page setting for both widgets: Automatic, Light or Dark. Automatic follows the Home Screen's light or dark mode; Light and Dark fix the page colour, which can look better with glassy icons.
- New: Previous Day, "A random day": a shuffle button jumps to a different random drawing.
- Improved: Previous Day: the Day setting only appears where it does something. With "Cycle through favourites" it lists only favourites, and the cycle starts from the one you pick.
- Fixed: Drawings now show on glassy (Clear and Tinted) Home Screens. The Today widget could show a blank white card.
- Fixed: The Previous Day widget no longer goes blank when set to a dark page.
{% endbuild %}

{% build 1, "2026-09-28" %}
- New: First TestFlight build: daily pages with a grace period, the gallery (grid, list and sketchbook), favourites, prompts and reminders, export and sharing, Year in Bloom, widgets, alternate app icons, and iCloud sync.
- New: DoodleBloom Premium (the Studio and time-lapse replays) and the tip jar.
{% endbuild %}
