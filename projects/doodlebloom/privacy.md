# Privacy Policy

**DoodleBloom**
_Last updated: 9 October 2026_

DoodleBloom is a daily drawing app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use DoodleBloom, including
DoodleBloom Premium and the tip jar.

## The short version

- **We don't collect your personal data.** DoodleBloom has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics,
  no advertising, and no tracking. It contains no third-party SDKs.
- **Your drawings are yours.** They're stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own
  private iCloud storage. We can't see them.
- **Apple handles all payments.** We never receive your name, email address, or payment details.

## What DoodleBloom stores, and where

DoodleBloom stores the things you create and the choices you make in the app:

- your daily drawings, and the images made from them
- page details such as titles, favourites, the page template, and the day's drawing prompt
- Studio pieces (copies of drawings you continue working on)
- your settings, such as reminder times, the grace period, your gallery layout, your app icon, and
  your tint colour

This information is stored **on your device**. It's also kept in a storage area shared with the
DoodleBloom widgets on the same device, so they can show your drawings on your Home Screen.

### iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, DoodleBloom syncs your drawings and Studio pieces between your
devices using Apple's CloudKit, in **your private iCloud database**. This data belongs to your
iCloud account. We, the developer, have no access to it: we can't read it, copy it, or share it.
Apple protects and processes iCloud data under
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for DoodleBloom in **Settings › [your name] › iCloud**.

## Permissions DoodleBloom may ask for

- **Notifications:** only if you turn on reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device, and
  their text (including the day's drawing prompt, if prompts are on) never leaves it. We don't
  send marketing or promotional notifications. iCloud sync may use Apple's silent push
  notifications to let your devices know there's something new to sync. These are never shown
  to you and carry no personal information.
- **Adding to Photos:** only when you choose to save a drawing to your photo library.
  DoodleBloom asks for "add only" access, so it can save images but can't see or read your
  photos.

## Sharing and exporting

When you share or export a drawing, a time-lapse replay, or a Year in Bloom poster, DoodleBloom
creates the file on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes.
From then on, the app or service you share with handles it under its own privacy policy.
DoodleBloom deletes its temporary copies of these files once you're done with them.

### Backups

A backup, made in **Settings › Back up and restore**, is a single file containing your drawings,
Studio pieces, and app settings. It's saved wherever you choose, such as your device, iCloud
Drive, or another storage service, and DoodleBloom never uploads it anywhere itself. Anyone you
give the file to can see your drawings. Restoring reads a backup file you pick, and only on your
device.

## DoodleBloom Premium and the tip jar

### How purchases work

All purchases are made through Apple's App Store and processed by Apple under
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We don't receive your name,
email address, Apple Account, or payment information.

DoodleBloom uses Apple's StoreKit framework on your device to check whether you have an active
subscription. That check happens between your device and Apple. Apple provides developers with
sales and subscription reports that don't identify individual customers.

### DoodleBloom Premium (auto-renewable subscription)

- **What it includes:** the Studio (copy any day's drawing and keep working on it) and
  time-lapse replays of your drawings.
- **Price:** $2.99 (USD) per month, or the equivalent price in your region as shown in the app
  before you buy.
- **Free trial:** new subscribers may be offered a 1-week free trial. If you're offered a trial,
  your subscription starts, and you're charged, when the trial ends, unless you cancel before
  then.
- **Payment:** payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when
  the free trial ends.
- **Automatic renewal:** your subscription renews automatically each month unless you cancel it
  at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal
  within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
- **Managing or cancelling:** manage or cancel your subscription at any time in
  **Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions** on your device. If you cancel, Premium stays
  active until the end of the period you've paid for.
- **If Premium ends:** your Studio pieces stay on your devices and in your iCloud. You can still
  view and export them. Editing them and making new replays needs an active subscription.

### Tips

The tip jar offers optional one-time purchases (Sketch Tip, Doodle Tip, and Masterpiece Tip).
Tips are a thank-you and don't unlock any features. Like all App Store purchases, they're
processed by Apple.

### Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. To request one, visit
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Information Apple may share with us

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings
(**Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements**), Apple may give us crash reports and
aggregated usage statistics. Apple doesn't identify you in them, and we use them only to find and
fix problems in DoodleBloom. You can turn this sharing off in the same place at any time.

### Beta testing with TestFlight

If you test a DoodleBloom beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares with us the feedback you
choose to send, including any screenshots and comments, and crash reports from the beta. Apple
handles TestFlight under its own terms and
[privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and you can stop testing at any time in
the TestFlight app. We use what beta testers send only to improve DoodleBloom.

## Children

DoodleBloom isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal
information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal data, there's
none to collect from children either.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- **Delete a page:** you can delete any day or Studio piece in the app. If you use iCloud, it's
  deleted from your other devices too.
- **Delete everything on a device:** deleting DoodleBloom removes its data from that device.
- **Delete your iCloud data:** go to **Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account
  Storage**, choose DoodleBloom, and delete its data.
- **Delete a backup:** backup files are yours, wherever you saved them. Delete them there, like any
  other file.

Since we never receive your data, we have nothing to keep or delete on our side, apart from any
email you send us.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as the right to
access, correct, or delete it. For example, these rights may come from the GDPR in the EU and UK,
or the CCPA in California. Because DoodleBloom doesn't collect or store personal data on our
side, we hold no personal data about you. We don't sell or share personal information, and never
have. If you have any questions about your rights, please contact us.

## Security

Your data stays in places Apple protects: your device (with iOS data protection) and your private
iCloud storage. DoodleBloom doesn't send your data to any server of ours, because we don't have
one.
