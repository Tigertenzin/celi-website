## 1.0 — In beta

{% build 5, "2026-10-08" %}
- Improved: In the background, the app keeps waiting through iCloud's retries until an upload succeeds, instead of stopping at the first failed attempt.
- Improved: Settings › iCloud sync explains why an upload failed, and the log can be shared as text.
{% endbuild %}

{% build 4, "2026-10-08" %}
- Improved: Automatic visits and widget captures get more time to reach iCloud in the background, and a second chance a few minutes later, so they're more likely to be on your iPad without opening your iPhone first.
- New: Settings › iCloud sync shows what each device has uploaded and downloaded, and when.
{% endbuild %}

{% build 3, "2026-10-05" %}
- New: Apple Watch complications, in every size a watch face offers: Capture, and Today, which shows how many places you've logged today and the last one. Tapping either logs where you are.
- Improved: The watch app is simpler: a capture button and today's list. Tapping a place shows the nearby places to name it by, with places still to name marked "To review".
- Improved: The watch app uses the same tint colour as your iPhone.
- Improved: The Places page is gone from the watch; saved places stay on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
{% endbuild %}

{% build 2, "2026-10-05" %}
- New: The iPad shows a day in two columns: the list beside a map of the day, or beside the waypoint you've picked.
- New: Automatic visits show on one map of the day, with how long you stayed at each.
- New: A saved place has its own page, with a Log a visit button and your recent visits.
- New: The Map tab shows today's waypoints, and opens on where you've been when your location isn't ready yet.
- Improved: Places you log from a widget, Control Center or automatic visits now sync to your other devices in the background, without opening the app first. Widgets refresh when new places arrive.
- Improved: A waypoint's page puts the name first, with suggestions right under it; the time, coordinates and source are grouped under Details.
- Improved: Logging a saved place is its own button, with Undo. Tapping a place opens it.
- Improved: Clearer wording: Obsidian links, Obsidian properties and the other formats each have a one-line description, and "Skip" is now "Hide from export".
- Improved: Distances are rounded sensibly (90 ft, 0.4 mi), with round choices in Settings.
- Improved: History shows each day in two lines, with its status in words.
- Improved: The Export sheet has labelled Copy and Share buttons under the preview.
- Improved: Settings is split into Capture, Automatic visits and Export pages.
- Improved: Empty screens offer a way to start, and the introduction is shorter.
- Fixed: Opening a waypoint could change its time when it was logged ahead of the device's clock.
- Fixed: Tapping a saved place to look at it logged a visit.
{% endbuild %}

{% build 1, "2026-10-02" %}
- New: First TestFlight build, for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac in one purchase.
- New: One-press capture from the app, widgets, Control Center, the Lock Screen, the Action button, Siri and Shortcuts, with repeat presses in the same spot merged.
- New: Nearby place suggestions, Load more places, a draggable pin to correct a position, and saved places.
- New: Optional automatic visits, kept hidden until you review them, and the Today's Map widget with an evening reminder to review.
- New: Export as Obsidian Map View links, Markdown, a table, CSV, GeoJSON or your own template, and a Shortcuts action that returns a day's export.
- New: The Mac app: review automatic visits from every day in one place, and add a day to your Obsidian daily note, either written in or opened for you to paste.
- New: iCloud sync for your journal and settings, a journal day that ends when you choose, keyboard shortcuts on iPad and Mac, and six app icons.
{% endbuild %}
