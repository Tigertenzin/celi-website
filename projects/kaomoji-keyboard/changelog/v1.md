## 1.0 — In beta

{% build 1, "2026-10-08" %}
- New: First TestFlight build, for iPhone and iPad.
- New: The KaoBloom keyboard: over 37,000 kaomoji in 536 categories, browsed as a grid with an A–Z index, and swiped through one category to the next.
- New: Favorites, Recent and Frequent in the keyboard, with long-press to favorite.
- New: Moods: tap a feeling in the keyboard and get matching kaomoji, optionally picked fresh each time by Apple Intelligence.
- New: The app: browse and search every kaomoji, filter and sort, hide ones you never want to see in the keyboard, and see your most-used kaomoji and categories in Stats.
- New: Smart Search: describe what you want ("a bear giving a hug") and Apple Intelligence finds it, on your device. Save a search, and it appears in the keyboard as a mood.
- New: KaoBloom Premium: create your own kaomoji, and build custom categories that show first in the keyboard.
- New: A tip jar, twelve accent colors, and an introduction that explains the optional Full Access permission before iOS asks.
{% endbuild %}
