## Description

KaoBloom puts kaomoji, the Japanese-style emoticons like (◕‿◕) and ʕ•ᴥ•ʔ, in a keyboard you can use in any app. Switch to it with the globe key, tap a face, and it's typed. No copying and pasting from a website.

**Over 37,000 kaomoji, in the keyboard**

- 536 categories, from Happy and Animals to Lenny and Dongers, browsed as a grid with an A–Z index
- Swipe left or right inside a category to move to the next one
- Favorites, Recent and Frequent, one tap away; long-press any kaomoji to favorite it
- Moods: tap a feeling like Sleepy, Celebrate or Shy, and get kaomoji to match

**Find the right face**

- Search every kaomoji by its characters or its category
- Smart Search: describe what you want, like "a bear giving a hug" or "table flip rage", and Apple Intelligence finds it, entirely on your device
- Save a search you like, and it shows up in the keyboard as a mood

**Make it yours**

- Hide the kaomoji you never want to see in the keyboard
- See your most-used kaomoji and categories in Stats
- Choose the keyboard's default page and one of twelve accent colors

**KaoBloom Premium**

- Custom kaomoji: create and keep your own
- Custom categories: group the kaomoji you use together, and they show first in your keyboard

Everything else is free, with an optional tip jar if you'd like to say thanks.

**Private by design**

- The keyboard never reads what you type, and makes no network requests at all
- Full Access is optional: it only lets the keyboard save your Recent and Stats on your device, and the keyboard works without it
- The app shares anonymous feature-usage counts, never what you type, your custom kaomoji or your searches, and you can turn that off in Settings
