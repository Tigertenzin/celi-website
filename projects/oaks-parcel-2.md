## Description

Oak's Parcel is a Pokédex for the main-line games from Generation I to VII, rebuilt for iPhone and iPad. Pick the game you're playing, and the Pokédex, moves, locations and type matchups follow it. It's free, with no ads and no features behind a paywall. (The first version of Oak's Parcel is [archived here](/projects/oaks-parcel/).)

**A Pokédex for the game you're playing**

- The first 807 Pokémon with sprites and forms, their national and regional dex numbers, stats as bars or a hexagon, type matchups, evolutions, abilities and moves for the selected game
- Every move, ability, item and location, with encounters
- Search by name or by national or regional dex number, and filter by type and generation
- On iPad, every list fills the screen as a table or a grid, and a sidebar takes you anywhere

**Shiny hunting**

- Count encounters, resets or eggs with the odds for your game and method, including the Shiny Charm and chaining
- Hunts with several targets, phases, notes, and a widget that counts from the Home Screen

**Teams and checklists**

- Build teams or import them from Pokémon Showdown, and share them as text or a QR code
- See each team's shared weaknesses and coverage, using the type chart of its game
- Track a living dex or a regional dex one PC box at a time

**Make it yours**

- A trainer card on Home in any game's color, with the counts, hunt, team and checklist you choose
- Themes for the whole app, widgets, and keyboard shortcuts on iPad
- Everything syncs between your devices with iCloud
- Private by design: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no data collected
