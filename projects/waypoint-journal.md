## Description

Waypoint Journal remembers where you've been, so your journal doesn't have to rely on your memory. One press saves where you are. Later, when you sit down to write, you tidy up the day and add it to your notes.

**One press, wherever you are**

- Capture from the app, a widget, Control Center, the Lock Screen, the Action button, Siri or Shortcuts, without opening the app
- An Apple Watch app for capturing from your wrist, with your saved places a tap away
- Pressed twice in the same spot? It updates the waypoint instead of adding a duplicate

**Find out what the place was**

- Each waypoint suggests what's nearby, nearest first, with the street address as a fallback
- Not quite right? Load more places, drag the pin to where you really were, or type your own name
- Save the places you go often, and log them in one tap

**Automatic visits, if you want them**

- Your iPhone can notice the places you stop at on its own
- They stay hidden until you review them: include the ones you want, and keep the rest out of your journal
- The Today's Map widget shows the day's places, and reminds you to review them in the evening

**Into your journal**

- Export a day as Obsidian Map View links, Markdown, a table, CSV, GeoJSON or your own template
- Copy it, share it, or fetch it from Shortcuts
- On the Mac, add a day straight into your Obsidian daily note, or open the note and paste it exactly where you want it

**On all your devices**

- iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac, kept in sync with iCloud
- Days end when you go to bed, not at midnight: you choose when a new day starts
- Keyboard shortcuts on iPad and Mac
- Private by design: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no data collected
