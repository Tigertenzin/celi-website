# Privacy Policy

**Oak's Parcel**
_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

Oak's Parcel is a Pokédex app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information
when you use it.

## The short version

- **We don't collect your personal data.** Oak's Parcel has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no
  advertising, and no tracking. It contains no third-party SDKs.
- **What you make is yours.** Your favorites, teams, hunts and checklists are stored on your devices and,
  if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. We can't see them.
- **The Pokédex is built in.** All of the Pokémon data ships inside the app, so looking things up never
  sends anything over the internet.

## What Oak's Parcel stores, and where

Oak's Parcel stores the things you save and the choices you make in the app:

- your favorite Pokémon, moves, abilities and items
- your teams, including sets imported from Pokémon Showdown
- your shiny hunts: their targets, game, method, counts, phases, dates and notes
- your checklists and the Pokémon you've marked as caught
- your trainer card: its trainer ID, Pokémon, color, and what it shows
- your settings, such as your game, theme and Home layout, and the Pokémon you viewed recently

This information is stored **on your device**. It's also kept in a storage area shared with the Oak's
Parcel widgets on the same device, so they can show your hunts, teams and trainer card, and count a hunt
from the Home Screen.

### iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Oak's Parcel syncs your favorites, teams, hunts and checklists between your
devices using Apple's CloudKit, in **your private iCloud database**, and syncs your settings and trainer
card using iCloud key-value storage. This data belongs to your iCloud account. We, the developer, have no
access to it: we can't read it, copy it, or share it. Apple protects and processes iCloud data under
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for Oak's Parcel in **Settings › [your name] › iCloud**.

## Permissions Oak's Parcel may ask for

- **Camera:** only if you choose to scan a team's QR code. The camera image is read on your device to find
  the code, and is never saved or sent anywhere.

iCloud sync may use Apple's silent push notifications to let your devices know there's something new to
sync. These are never shown to you and carry no personal information.

## Tips

Oak's Parcel is free. If you leave a tip, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store.
We never see your payment details, and we don't learn who you are from it.

## Sharing and links

When you share a team, Oak's Parcel creates its text or QR code on your device. You choose where it goes,
and from then on the app or service you send it to handles it under its own privacy policy.

Links in the app, such as to our website or the data sources we credit, open in your browser and leave
the app. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

## Information Apple may share with us

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings (on iPhone,
**Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements**), Apple may give us crash reports and
aggregated usage statistics. Apple doesn't identify you in them, and we use them only to find and fix
problems in Oak's Parcel. You can turn this sharing off in the same place at any time.

TestFlight testers may also send us feedback and screenshots through TestFlight. We only receive what you
choose to send, and use it only to improve the app.

## Children

Oak's Parcel isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information
from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal data, there's none to collect from
children either.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- **Delete a team, hunt, checklist or favorite:** you can delete any of them in the app. If you use
  iCloud, they're deleted from your other devices too.
- **Delete everything on a device:** deleting Oak's Parcel removes its data from that device.
- **Delete your iCloud data:** go to **Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage**, choose
  Oak's Parcel, and delete its data.

Since we never receive your data, we have nothing to keep or delete on our side.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as the right to access,
correct, or delete it. For example, these rights may come from the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in
California. Because Oak's Parcel doesn't collect or store personal data on our side, we hold no personal
data about you. We don't sell or share personal information, and never have. If you have any questions
about your rights, please contact us.

## Security

Your data stays in places Apple protects: your devices (with Apple's data protection) and your private
iCloud storage. Oak's Parcel doesn't send your data to any server of ours, because we don't have one.
