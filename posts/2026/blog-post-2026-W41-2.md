Over a week without writing a blog post. Why? Well, partially because of this: 
![](/posts/attachments/blog-post-2026-w41-2/pasted-image-20261015165402.jpeg)

Over the weekend, some friends and I hiked up Mt. Chocorua, this big mountain in New Hampshire. From the trails we took, it ended up being 10.45 miles, with around 4,000 ft of elevation gain (at least according to my Apple Watch). It was by far the longest hike I’ve ever done. 

But more importantly, being at the top of that mountain was so serene, peaceful. It’s one thing to look down from an airplane. It’s so different, feeling the cool air and truly feeling that height, peaking over the cliff edges. 

![](/posts/attachments/blog-post-2026-w41-2/pasted-image-20261015174228.jpeg)

For those curious, I used the Dazz app, with the “Insta C” filter(?). I like the film grain and the lighter, more washed-out colors. 

## not touching grass 

That’s this past weekend. But it’s also already almost the end of the week. Work has been too busy, and I’ve been too tired to write. 

It doesn’t help that I’ve been sacrificing sleep to instead read [The Emperor and the Goddess](https://archiveofourown.org/works/23064598/chapters/55167319), an incredibly long and well-written *Fire Emblem Three Houses* fanfic. 

I’ve got a bigger project almost ready to put out to the public. Then, maybe someone will see it and eventually find these emblems at this blog, and maybe even these words! That’d be fun! I just have to get out of my own head, and remember that "perfect is the enemy of good.”
