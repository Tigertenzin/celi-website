I preordered *Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave Dagdan Collection* (aka, the game with a bunch of special edition things) the second it went up for preorder, and it finally arrived (a day late)! i’m so happy and excited to sink my teeth into this game! I Love Fire Emblem! 
![](/posts/attachments/pasted-image-20260918190203.jpg) ![](/posts/attachments/pasted-image-20260918190420.jpg)

i gushed about this game a bit in [my recent instagram post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdceL9bnTg-/?stkn=dHh0Y242ZG03dnlv), but in summary: i was just so excited about everything related to this. Sothis, i’m ready! 

i put a good 10-15 or so hours into this game so far, and have some thoughts already. like, first of all, the presentation of this game is outstanding. the world building, the characters (that i’ve met and recruited so far), and the vibes are just incredible. the introduction is a bit crazy, very unlike tradition fire emblem, but in a fun way that feels fresh but not necessarily out of place. then getting started with one of the lords, it kinda felt like getting right back to *Three Houses*.

I started with Leda, mostly since i wasn’t super sold on any of the 4 lords. but seeing snippets of gameplay and trailers where she’s looking and acting a big crazy and unhinged, i felt inspired! like, look at this! 
![](/posts/attachments/pasted-image-20260922122058.jpg)

but i’m definitely going to go back through every single route. some estimates i was seeing online put that at over 100 hours of playtime so… i’m in for the long haul! 

that being said, so far i’m cautiously optimistic about this game. like with Three Houses, one of my biggest gripes is the amount of time you spend doing chores, scrambling around to micro-optimize your units’s stats/skills and building supports with other units to recruit more to your army. this, so far, has taken up a lot of my playtime, possibly even more so than the “actual Fire Emblem gameplay,” as some purists call it, of the grid-based turn based combat. that’s my favorite part of Fire Emblem games, so i can see where theyre coming from, and so far the maps at the end of chapters haven’t wowed me. i enjoyed them, but i still want to play more before i have any sort of conclusive opinion. 

TL:DR, i’m enjoying my time with this game and excited to see what else it has in store! 
 
## the other things 

i was continuing *Xenoblade Chronicles Definitive Edition* earlier last week. i’ve really enjoyed my playthrough and my first foray into the xenoblade series. it’s so highly regarded, including by my partner, and i can understand why. i was actually hoping to finish it before Fortune’s Weave released, but that didn’t end up happening, so now it’s gonna go on an extended hiatus. 

i also read this novel, *Annie On My Mind* by Nancy Garden. it’s kind of a classic, i think, at least in the coming-of-age queer stories. and it was so pretty, so beautiful, i enjoyed it so much. it’s a story about two girls finding love, feeling guilty about the way they love, and trying to accept who they are. i wouldn’t call it unpredictable, but it is a lovely read. i’m always highlighting pretty sounding lines, or really anything that stands out while i read, and this one i thought was worth sharing: 

> The important part is the wonder of the closeness and the unbearable ultimate realization that we are two people, not one—and also the wonder of that: that even though we are two people, we can be almost like one, and at the same time delight in each other’s uniqueness. ... We can be almost like one …

## concluding 

time to no life *Fortune’s Weave*! I was so tempted to take some time off work to no-life this game, but i think i’ll be responsible and save my sick/vacation time for when i really need it. happy Fortune’s Weaving!
