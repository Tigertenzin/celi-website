I started my blog in a pretty eventful time for my hobbies. Apple Events, TCG Set Releases, Video Game releases. But now, things have calmed down, so what do I write about now? There can always be something exciting going on, if i choose to be excited for it! 

## enjoying free time (not just in Dagsion)

i've continued playing *Fire Emblem Fortune's Weave*, cus of course i have if you read my last blog post. i haven't had as much time as i'd like to play, but i've made it through several more chapters in Leda's route, and my thoughts haven't really changed. I'm enjoying this game overall. But the free time takes up a lot of my playtime, and the end-of-chapter battles (the part i'm mainly here for) are a bit too easy for my taste. I also feel like I've seen so little of what the game has to offer, so more than anything, i'm excited to continue playing more, maybe during the weekend (and the Nor'easter we have forecasted)

I also finally watched the film: *Get Out*. I'm so late to the party, but I really enjoyed this movie! It hits that perfect sweet spot for me of thriller and psychological horror, while not falling into this modern movie trap of stereotypical action and/or comedy. So, yeah, I loved it! 

I also started reading *Annihilation* by Jeff VanderMeer. And by started, I really do mean just started; barely started. Finding time to read with Fortune's Weave right there is… hard. But there are moments here and there, and I've heard a lot of great things about this novel, so I'm determined to give it a good shot! My understanding is that it's sci-fi and a kinda horror? But my real thoughts will be to come! 

## enjoying not-free time too! 

My real jobby job has been a bit busy as well, but at least there's time to hang out with all my cool coworkers! That's like, the only joy of being in-office, really. Hanging out, complaining about our jobs and workloads. Sharing common interests with each other. Keeps us all sane. 

Speaking of seeing the bright side of things, we got a new oven! My partner and I rent our apartment, and our old oven decided to give up a week ago. So our "generous" landlords bought us a whole new one! It’s still a gas stove, thankfully. Not that I have a choice in the matter anyways. They decide what to buy, and we're stuck with it. It still has no screen to know when the oven reaches a temperature or anything, and smells of burning plastic… so about what I’d expect. To be clear, it’s not a favor to us that they bought a new oven, since that’s an investment in the unit on their end, for when it gets rented out next. So, just have to enjoy it while we have it, I suppose.

It’s just me trying to put my (Apple) pencil to (digital) paper and seeing where my hand takes me. I’ve been creating a new canvas a day, and seeing where they end up. This is one that I’m particularly happy with. You'll see it's rather simple and random, and that's kind of the point. I don't want to think too hard about it; I just want to do it!
