## iPhone Duo

Secured my iPhone Duo pre-order this morning. Those delivery date estimates slid back to 2027 so quickly; I feel very lucky I got straight in, pressed the order button, and got my order confirmed notification. The system of basically setting up your order earlier in the week is so slick and smart. The ease with which it (usually) works is just so Apple. 

![](/posts/attachments/blog-post-2026-w42-2/pasted-image-20261016150442.jpg)

I went with the Night Sky (blue/black) color, almost exclusively because I saw most people online leaning towards the white/silver color, and I didn't want to worry about getting my pre-order in... and I didn’t really have a color preference. Neither are real colors… 

I have an additional 2 weeks of waiting from the actual release date. I've always chosen to pick up at an Apple Store, since dealing with signing packages in my apartment is very inconsistent/sketchy. But none of the stores in the Boston area seemed to have that as an option. Not having a car and having to go to a further store basically sealed my fate.
