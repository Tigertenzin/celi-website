## inspiration 

i've been writing little weekly blogposts to myself for the past year (with some several month-long hiatuses in the middle of it). I started it as a way to both work on my writing skills and reflect on the more enjoyable aspects of my life. the fun things happening, the cool and interesting pieces of media i consume, etc. 

This was inspired by [Myke Hurley's blog](https://www.theenthusiast.net), which i've been following since its conception (as a longtime Relay podcast fan). but i recently came across [this personal blog by Anh](https://anhvn.com), with a lot of different sections and super cute and cool looking web design, i was inspired to try doing something like that myself. maybe this could be a fun way to learn web design, but also to serve a purpose: a development portfolio. I previously used a WordPress site, which looked very amateurish, even when i first made it in 2020 when i first tried my hand at iOS app development (since then, i've decommissioned that site in favor of this new site, which i feel like i have much more control over). 

now, at the time of writing this, the site is incredibly barebones and basically has no content or styling. just a skeleton of links to my projects and these blogposts that i'll probably post sporadically, but in my head is a weekly endeavor. i would like the website to evolve and change as my tastes do, especially given its current form. but that will take time, and i imaging this site being something that never reaches a "final" state. 

## a little about me

so, who am i? a working adult with a full time job that never requires me to write code. i learned Python in undergrad, and picked up iOS development during the pandemic to make little projects for myself first. of course, i then put them out into the world, in case others have similar niche use cases like i do. 

what else? i spend all my extra money on pokemon cards. i post pretty pictures of them, typically among plants and nature (here's a [recent example](https://www.instagram.com/p/DaDZPd7lQGd/?igsi=YzdmaHJieWtienpy) below), on [my instagram account](https://www.instagram.com/celeryboi1). that's usually where i'm most active on the internet. but i do "exist" on [Mastodon](https://mastodon.world/@Celeryboi) and [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/celeryboi.bsky.social). i post other hobby stuff on my instagram, mostly video games, sometimes books. 
![Milotic ex (美纳斯ex) SAR - SV Sparkling Fable](/posts/attachments/an-introduction-pokemon-card.jpg)

i'm also… a terrible writer! in my opinion, at least. which is why i've been writing these, as well as journaling daily, for the past year or so. practice makes better, right? so hopefully through this process, i'll improve on that as well! 

## what next? 

well, hopefully i keep writing and posting to this blog. i'm still trying to figure out what exactly i want to post publicly. it's a bit nerve-racking to think about strangers reading my writing, even if its just a couple curious eyes. therefore, i'm not quite sure how much of my own opinions on things i'll feel comfortable sharing, especially at first. 

that being said, i have projects that i'm working on. those are on my projects page. i continue to post pretty pokemon card pictures and other nerdy stuff on my instagram. and maybe i'll even try posting on Mastodon and Bluesky. who knows! maybe this will be my only blog post, and it will lay abandoned and forgotten. only time will tell. 

see you next time! ヾ(＾∇＾)
