I mentioned previously that I've been trying to draw more, at least once a day. What I wanted was something simple, like the corners of your notebook you would use in class, where you could doodle something random, a bit mindlessly, just putting pen to paper. Being a big iPad user, I wanted a similar experience there. So I developed this app to help enable my daily drawings: **DoodleBloom**!

![](https://celeryboi.com/projects/attachments/doodlebloom/banner.webp)

The idea of the app is simple: every day, you start on a blank page. And then you draw something. Then the next day, flip to the next page and draw something new.

## Some technical details

I used the built-in PencilKit tools, primarily because it was the easiest option for me to develop with. But it also has the benefit of keeping it simple. I love Procreate as a tool for some more serious art. But these types of serious drawing apps, with all the different brushes and layers, can become overwhelming. I wanted to keep it as simple as possible to start drawing.

Using the built-in tools had several nice side effects as well. It enables you to easily switch between light and dark mode for drawings, which is handy since, at least for me, personally, I'll sometimes be drawing late at night, so having a dark canvas is a bit easier on the eyes. And it allows the app to remember your strokes, so that it can replay them later for you as a video. 

## No streaks to break

While the goal is to draw *something* every day, the idea of streaks and habit forming was something I considered. Streaks can be a good way to keep you motivated to come back. But they can also be stressful, and breaking a streak can be devastating to the forming habit. I wanted to avoid this, since I know that sometimes, life gets busy. So there's no penalty to not drawing that day, no streak to break. The only thing close is a gentle push notification to remind you to draw.

## Seeing everything come together

Sometimes, you just fixate on a piece of art you wanna keep working on. So, there's a "Studio" in the app for continuing with a drawing at a later date, and letting it continue to develop. 

There are some built-in sharing features. In addition to exporting a single drawing, there are some broader-view views. You can see your entire month or year of drawings, and export that automatically composed collage.

## In Conclusion

The page for this project is also on my website, see [here](https://celeryboi.com/projects/doodlebloom/) for more info and screenshots. 

I've been using the app for over a month as I've been developing and testing it, and I really like that it's pushed me to make more art. It's currently still in beta. I'm not sure I'm ready to release it fully yet, as there may still be bugs. So, if you're interested, please try it out and let me know what you think!
