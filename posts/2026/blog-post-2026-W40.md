I can’t believe it’s already October. September flew by so fast, what with work being so busy. but I’m glad it’s finally cooling down outside. I’ve really come to appreciate the fall weather, especially after several years of living in an apartment with only a single window AC unit. But also, sweaters, warm tea, and being cozy! 

While at work, I’ve been working on a bunch of coding projects on the side! nothing i’m quite ready to show yet, but spoilers, there’s an iOS app in my future. There is one thing i can talk about though! i don’t know if i’ll ever publish the code though. it’s just a small little thing. 

## Three Houses 

**To preface**: I’m one of those people that uses Obsidian religiously and loves to tinker with it as much as I like to do actual work within it. 

**Second preface**: *Fire Emblem: Three Houses* is my favorite game of all time. It’s why I was so excited for Fortune’s Weave. I love the blend of story, gameplay, and characters in Three Houses so much. So, I just wanted to gush about it here for a bit.

## Fortune’s Weave, and Other Media  

I’ve continued playing *Fire Emblem Fortune's Weave*, and continue to enjoy it. As the game has gone on, the difficulty has started going up, which I’m thankful for. It’s been a bit on the easy side so far, but people online are saying the difficulty really ramps up in the later parts of the game, which is especially exciting. And through it all, I’ve really enjoyed the main cast of characters on this route. 

I’ve also continued reading *Annihilation* by Jeff VanderMeer. I can’t tell if it’s just a slower and more difficult read, or if it’s just not my genre and style of book. But it’s been slow going. It could also just be that my brain is filled with Fire Emblem (and work) such that there’s not much left for reading. 

Manga, however, fills a different satisfactory hole in my brain, cus I just started *[Pluto](https://anilist.co/manga/30745/Pluto/)* earlier today and, wow, this manga is so good! Serious, sci-fi, crime, thriller. And I’m binge-reading it! I’m a bit late to the party; this series has been out for a long time. not to mention, i’ve been a light Astro Boy fan, when i played the GBA game as a kid. but i’m glad i’m getting to it now, it’s really good and i highly recommend.
