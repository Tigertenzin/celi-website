Yesterday was the official release date of the [30th Celebration set](https://tcg.pokemon.com/en-us/expansions/30th-celebration/), and it was the most fun I've had with a set in such a long time! The actual hit rate for the set is so much higher than the standard sets, and each booster pack has a cute pikachu card! [these were my hits](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdXa2voHQOG/?img_index=1), with more shots coming soon: 

![overview shot of all my hit cool pulls from the 30th celebration set. featuring Mew ex and Mewtwo ex](/posts/attachments/pasted-image-20260917094956.jpg)![a closeup shot of the special Pikachu cards in the 30th celebration set. ](/posts/attachments/pasted-image-20260917095019.jpg)

More broadly, it was so heartening seeing many of my fellow card collecting Instagram friends finding stuff to open in stores and seeing all their hits! Not to mention, many retailer stores had such large stocks that, from what I could tell online, so lots of people were actually able to purchase! It’s a bit sad that a lot of my European friends have to wait a bit longer (the release date there was delayed a couple of weeks), but they'll have their moment soon too! 

It’s been a while since I’ve had this much fun with a set. Lately, it's been so hard to find packs out in the wild, and the stuff you do find is sold by scalpers charging a 2x markup or more (yes, GameStop included). All the people out there looking to make a quick buck have furthered scarcity of sealed product, and driven up prices of singles on secondary market, making it almost prohibitively expensive, and it's sad. So the fact all signs are pointing at this beautiful set to crash hard from their hugely inflated presale prices is nice; it gives me hope. 

That’s all I wanted to say here. I wanted to dedicate a post specifically to the joy I had last night opening this set and sharing that joy with all my friends that were also opening this set. Here’s hoping this set remains accessible, cheap, and for the fans! 

Now, I'll continue to wait for my copy of *Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave* to arrive. I pre-ordered the Dagdan collection, so it's taking a little extra long to get here, not to mention we have friends staying over so I can't really no-life this game while they're here anyways.
