# this week was eventful  

interesting week where there were a couple of events that i found myself excited to watch! both were on the same day too, which seems rare (but maybe it’s not). 

earlier in the day was the Nintendo Direct. there’s always tons of rumors and “leaks” for what will be in the event, but there’s always a surprise or two in there. i was hopefully for a new Smash Bros. game, which sadly didn’t happen. but announcement for *new Metroid and Kirby games* are very exciting, even if a large part of the internet was disappointed. it’s ok to be disappointed, as long as its not at the expense of other people’s enjoyment. sadly that’s not a sentiment i see carried out by everyone. 

and then later, there was the Apple Event, “Surprise and Shine,” even though it wasn’t really a surprise at all. the big reveal was the folding iPhone, the *iPhone Duo*. the software details, however, did carry some surprise! it’s got that apple level of consideration for the hardware, the thing theyre best at that other phone manufacturers don’t quite grasp. suffice it to say, i will be preorder this phone as soon as i can, in whatever color is availed. sadly no fun colors, but at least i don’t have to stress to much about getting a color i want. 

the announcements that have me the most excited? the daily trickle of new characters they reveal for *Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave*. Every single day, Nintendo has been showing off a new character, a little blurb, a clip of them interacting with some previously revealed characters. it’s been so great to keep me into the hype-cycle of the game. Especially last wednesday, when they revealed Seteth, returning from *Three Houses*! Out of anything left this year, Fortune’s Weave is where my excitement is the highest. 

## other media 

is the **US Open** media, since i’m watching it? or is it an event, cus it’s… an event? in any case, i’ve been watching that! i was into tennis as a kid, and my partner is into tennis now, so it’s been fun getting back into it recently, playing it occasionally, but also watching the professionals go at it. im not a huge sports person and dont really watch any otherwise, but its fun to get invested in a game!

i read a new book, *The Goodman* by Clare Ashton. it’s a novel about romance, family, and secrets. it started off good and only got better as i’ve kept reading. it’s a bit frustrating at times, but that’s the risk you run reading a book about flawed characters. maybe i’ll sum up my thoughts in a more dedicated post or something, but for now, i liked it, and i would recommend!

the last thing big thing is that i’ve been playing *Xenoblade Chronicles: Definitive Edition - Switch 2 Edition*. the names gotten a bit ridiculous at this point, but it’s such a great JRPG. like, really great! i started it midway through the summer, hoping to finish it before Fortune’s Weave’s release next week, but it didn’t end up happening. i was busy, but it’s also such a long game. definitely check out at least the music, it’s some of the best OST in all of video games.
